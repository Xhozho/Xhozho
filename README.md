### 2. UrbanStyle Customer Segmentation | SQL

Explored how customer spending differed across UrbanStyle's customer base and cities, using SQL to group customers by spending and identify patterns in customer value.

- **Key Discovery:** Identified **206 VIP customers**, representing around **8% of customers**, who contributed **31.25% of customer revenue**.
- **What I Built:** Used SQL queries with CTEs, CASE WHEN, aggregations, and window functions to create spending-based customer segments and compare customer value across cities.
- **Business Value:** Found that while VIP customers contributed a significant share of revenue, Regular customers generated **60.60%**, highlighting the importance of considering both groups when planning customer retention strategies.

### 3. UrbanStyle Data Processing | Python & Pandas

Developed reusable Python functions to clean, transform, and analyse UrbanStyle's sales and customer data as part of a team-built data pipeline.

- **Key Discovery:** Validated **10,118 sales records**, identifying **988 missing customer IDs** and **195 negative-price records** that required further investigation.
- **What I Built:** Used Pandas to clean and merge datasets, calculate weekly sales summaries, and generate KPIs, including total revenue and average order value.
- **Business Value:** Checked that sales records remained consistent during processing and flagged potential data-quality issues that could affect reporting and business decisions.
